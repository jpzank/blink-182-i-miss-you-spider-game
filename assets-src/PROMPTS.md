## Painel e papel de parede
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high-resolution architectural texture for a first-person browser game inspired by the candlelit mansion salon in blink-182 I Miss You. Make a seamless horizontally repeating elevation of an antique Edwardian / Gothic Revival dark walnut wall, straight-on orthographic photograph, no perspective. Lower third: richly carved dark walnut wainscot panels with slender recessed rectangles, small acanthus details, aged patina and fine grain. Upper two thirds: faded dark olive damask wallpaper with subtle small motifs, age stains, irregular worn edges, very fine fabric surface. Crown molding along top, wood dado at lower third. Deep realistic sculptural relief, restrained tiny old brass details. Neutral soft diffuse frontal light with naturally baked crevice occlusion, no cast directional shadows. Square-on full frame texture, no room, no floor, no people, no furniture, no windows, no lights, no text, no margins. Landscape 3:2. Fine photographic detail, absolutely no cartoon or 3D toy aesthetic.

Exact orthographic top down photograph, the entire rectangular rug fills the image edge to edge, absolutely no floor or border outside the rug. Very intricate fine Persian floral arabesques, central medallion and multi-row traditional border, faded burgundy, dark indigo, muted parchment and olive wool, 100 years of wear, detailed thin woven fibres and abrasion, restrained contrast, dusty antique candlelit mansion decor. Diffuse flat neutral lighting, no perspective, no cast shadows, no objects, no text. Portrait 2:3 aspect.
