# Community roadmap

These are contribution directions, not promised release dates. Open an issue with a small, demonstrable improvement and discuss larger replacements first.

## Graphics

- Improve facial likeness and skin shading without relying on baked directional shadows.
- Refine hair silhouettes, fabric folds, hands and contact with pillows and instruments.
- Give the Sphynx more natural anatomy and grooming, with fewer triangles where possible.
- Improve mansion lighting, contact shadows and materials under the low spider camera.
- Explore mesh/texture compression and level of detail while measuring loading and rendering on real phones.

## Gameplay

- Make crawling, jumping and transitions between surfaces feel more natural.
- Clarify the three-thread route while rewarding exploration of the alcove and garden.
- Make cat detection and checkpoint feedback more readable and fair.
- Improve touch-camera control and offer useful sensitivity settings.
- Test camera comfort and accessibility with people who play the game.

## Good first contributions

- Document a reproducible visual or collision issue with a location and short capture.
- Improve one material or small prop, showing before/after views in the game.
- Test on a physical mobile device and report loading time, frame rate, browser and hardware.
- Improve English instructions where a new player gets lost.
- Add a focused regression test for a confirmed progression or music-control bug.
